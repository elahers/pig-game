🐷 Pig Game

  A simple and addictive dice game where players roll, hold, and try to reach 100 points before their opponent!

🎮 How to Play

  Roll the dice as many times as you like each turn.

  If you roll a 1, your turn ends and you lose points for that turn.

  Choose Hold to keep your points and pass the turn.

  First to 100 points wins!
